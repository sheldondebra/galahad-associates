Galahad Associates provides tailored security and risk management support to businesses, government agencies, non-profits and private clients worldwide.

Our backgrounds span military service, UK Special Forces and the corporate sector. That breadth helps us understand both the security challenge and the environment in which you need to operate.

We believe effective security starts with listening. Your people, priorities and circumstances shape the support we recommend, from strategic advice to practical protection.
